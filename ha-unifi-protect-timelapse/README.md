# UniFi Protect Timelapse (Home Assistant app)

Timelapse capture and archive watchdog for a UniFi Protect camera. By default it
captures nothing: it watches that the camera answers and that images keep
landing in your archive, and gives you the live frame, the archive browser and
video export inside Home Assistant.

Source, documentation and standalone Docker deployment:
<https://github.com/steiner-dominik/unifi-protect-timelapse>
